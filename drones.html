I was watching this video: Antenna Mod Tutorial the other day, and saw the use of a cable tie to help point the antenna down instead of straight out like it comes from the factory to gain extra range.

On a spur of the moment decision, I decided to give it a try. I for the most part followed the directions in the video, and then just kept going, adding ties to each arm. Then, using the short leg where the antenna was tied, I clipped the rest to match. Using the remainder of two of the ties I created skids, and using two more, I made them more stable by adding wide cross bars. I enhanced the visibility of the rear red LED's, red propellers by adding red cable ties to the back end.

Assembly Note: Content relies heavily on secure cable ties, structurally bound by hot glue joints.
